# Letter of Recommendation

[Date]

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend **Md. Sakib Al Hasan** (Student ID: 12011064) for graduate study in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or a related discipline. I have known Sakib since the Winter 2020 session at the University of Global Village, where I taught him and supervised his undergraduate thesis.

His thesis, **“Guava Leaf Disease Detection and Classification Based on Transfer Learning Technique,”** required sustained work with data, machine-learning models, and experimental evaluation. He studied guava-leaf images representing seven classes and investigated transfer-learning models for disease classification. The Xception model achieved a reported test accuracy of 95.54%.

During the project, Sakib demonstrated persistence in the less visible parts of applied research: preparing data, maintaining consistency between experiments, interpreting results, and explaining limitations. He completed the thesis in November 2023 and defended it in February 2024. His performance showed that he could work through a technically demanding project and communicate the outcome clearly.

His professional experience as a Product Design Engineer has added practical depth to his computing background. By working with engineers and web developers, he translates requirements into implementable decisions and considers usability, reliability, and real-world constraints. I believe he is prepared for graduate study and recommend him for admission and scholarship consideration.
