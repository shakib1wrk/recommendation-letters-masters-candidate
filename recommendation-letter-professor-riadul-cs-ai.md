# Letter of Recommendation — Computer Science / AI

[Date]

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend **Md. Sakib Al Hasan** (Student ID: 12011064) for research-focused graduate study in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or Machine Learning. I have known him since the Winter 2020 session at the University of Global Village, where I taught him and co-supervised his undergraduate thesis, **“Guava Leaf Disease Detection and Classification Based on Transfer Learning Technique.”**

The thesis involved applying transfer learning to guava-leaf disease classification. Sakib worked with image data spanning seven classes and evaluated multiple approaches; Xception achieved a reported test accuracy of 95.54%. The research required attention to dataset preparation, model evaluation, and the interpretation of results. He completed the thesis in November 2023 and defended it in February 2024.

My assessment of Sakib is grounded in the way he engaged with the research process. He persisted through data and experimental tasks, worked to keep the comparisons meaningful, and explained the outcome of the project. These are useful habits for a student moving into more advanced work in computing, where the quality of a conclusion depends on the care taken in producing and evaluating evidence.

Since completing his degree, Sakib has worked as a Product Design Engineer. His collaboration with software engineers and web developers gives him experience translating requirements into implementable product decisions. It also exposes him to the practical concerns of software delivery, including usability and real-world constraints.

Sakib has a CSE foundation, applied AI research experience, and a clear motivation to continue learning. I recommend him for admission and scholarship consideration in a research-oriented computing or AI program.
