Sakib had to consider these factors, explain his decisions, and respond to questions at his February 2024 thesis defense. That process gave me confidence in his capacity to continue developing as a researcher.

Sakib has a sound foundation for advanced study and the capacity to strengthen it through graduate research. I recommend him for admission and scholarship consideration in computing and AI programs.
