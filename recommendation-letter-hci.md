**To the Admissions Committee,**

I am pleased to recommend **Md. Sakib Al Hasan** for admission to your master’s program in **Human-Computer Interaction, User Experience Research, or Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence**. I taught Sakib and supervised his undergraduate thesis in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the **University of Global Village, Barishal**. I have known him since he began university in the **Winter 2020 session**. As a member of the 6th batch and a student with ID 12011064, he demonstrated a thoughtful interest in how computing can address practical human needs.

Since completing his undergraduate studies, Sakib has developed substantial professional experience in UX and product design. He currently works as a **Product Design Engineer at WolfDevs**, a Bangladesh-based company building no-code e-commerce and web products. His work includes UX research, end-to-end product design, collaboration with stakeholders and software engineers, production-ready frontend implementation, and design-system work. Previously, as a Senior UX Designer and UX/UI Designer, he led complete UX cycles involving research, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes, interface design, usability testing, and developer handoff. His professional direction is therefore strongly consistent with graduate study in HCI, UX research, interaction design, and human-centered AI.

Sakib’s preparation for HCI-oriented graduate work is grounded in both computing and applied problem solving. During his undergraduate studies, he completed the thesis **“Guava Leaf Disease Detection and Classification Based on Transfer Learning Technique.”** This project required him to engage with a real-world problem, understand the needs of an intended user community, work with imperfect visual data, and translate a technical method into a practical decision-support context. In my view, this experience is especially relevant to human-centered AI because successful intelligent systems must be not only accurate, but also understandable, usable, and appropriate for the people who rely on them.

What distinguishes Sakib is his interest in the relationship between technical systems and their users. In his professional work, he has translated business and stakeholder needs into user-centered, developer-ready solutions across SaaS, e-commerce, finance, WordPress, Chrome-extension, and web-builder products. His portfolio documents detailed case studies including Onnomatra, FinlyPal, and OmniPixCraft. In the Onnomatra case study, he describes designing an e-commerce platform for Bangladeshi sellers and identifies contributions in user research, 5W2H analysis, personas, user flows, information architecture, product strategy, mind mapping, wireframing, and UI design. The OmniPixCraft case study focuses on automating Open Graph image creation for modern WordPress websites and identifies contributions in user research, ideation, style-guide development, user flows, information architecture, and user-interface design. These projects demonstrate that he can consider different users, domains, constraints, and interaction contexts. These are important foundations for UX research and human-centered AI, where strong practitioners combine methodological discipline with attention to the people who experience and rely on a system.

Sakib also showed the capacity to learn unfamiliar concepts and apply them independently. His thesis used 3,240 guava-leaf photographs collected primarily at Swarupkathi Guava Garden in Pirojpur, with images captured using several smartphone cameras. He prepared the images at 512 × 512 pixels, organized them into seven classes, applied augmentation, and compared transfer-learning models including Xception, Inception V3, VGG16, and VGG19. The best-performing model, Xception, achieved 95.54% test accuracy. Just as importantly, he identified limitations involving dataset size, disease similarity, and generalization. Together with his professional use of user research, information architecture, prototyping, design systems, Figma, FigJam, and frontend technologies including React and Astro, this combination of technical curiosity and reflective problem solving will serve him well in graduate research.

In addition to his technical ability, Sakib has developed a clear professional identity in product design and interaction design. His portfolio describes his approach as understanding real user problems, turning them into simple and useful digital experiences, and collaborating with stakeholders and engineers. His thesis was completed in November 2023 and defended in February 2024. He communicates respectfully, takes responsibility for his work, and engages seriously with feedback and revision. I believe these qualities will help him contribute positively to a diverse research group and engage seriously with the ethical and social dimensions of AI and interactive technology.

I recommend Md. Sakib Al Hasan for admission with confidence. He combines a CSE foundation, a research experience in applied AI, and approximately three years of professional UX design experience. This combination gives him a valuable perspective for graduate work that connects user research, interaction design, and intelligent systems. I expect him to grow into a capable HCI or human-centered AI researcher and to make meaningful contributions to graduate study.
