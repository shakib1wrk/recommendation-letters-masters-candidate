**To the Admissions Committee,**

I am pleased to recommend **Md. Sakib** for admission to your master’s program in **Human-Computer Interaction, User Experience Research, or Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence**. I know Sakib through his undergraduate study in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the **University of Global Village, Barishal**, where he was a member of the 6th batch and held student ID 12011064. I have known him since he began university in 2020, both as his course teacher and as his undergraduate thesis supervisor. I consider him a thoughtful, motivated, and promising candidate for graduate study at the intersection of technology and human needs.

Since completing his undergraduate studies, Sakib has also worked as a **UX Designer for approximately three years**. This professional experience has given him practical exposure to understanding users, shaping digital experiences, communicating design decisions, and connecting technology with real human needs. His professional direction is consistent with his proposed graduate interests in HCI, UX design and research, and human-centered AI. He has presented examples of this work through his portfolio at **portfolio.sakib.design**.

Sakib’s preparation for HCI-oriented graduate work is grounded in both computing and applied problem solving. During his undergraduate studies, he completed the thesis **“Guava Leaf Disease Detection and Classification Based on Transfer Learning Technique.”** This project required him to engage with a real-world problem, understand the needs of an intended user community, work with imperfect visual data, and translate a technical method into a practical decision-support context. In my view, this experience is especially relevant to human-centered AI because successful intelligent systems must be not only accurate, but also understandable, usable, and appropriate for the people who rely on them.

What distinguishes Sakib is his interest in the relationship between technical systems and their users. His thesis explicitly considered early detection, interpretability, real-time prediction, ethical deployment, and the value of an agricultural decision-support tool for farmers. He connected the technical goal of classification with the practical question of how disease predictions could support timely and informed crop-care decisions. These are important foundations for UX research and human-centered AI, where strong practitioners combine methodological discipline with attention to the people who experience and rely on a system.

Sakib also showed the capacity to learn unfamiliar concepts and apply them independently. His thesis used 3,240 guava-leaf photographs collected primarily at Swarupkathi Guava Garden in Pirojpur, with images captured using several smartphone cameras. He prepared the images at 512 × 512 pixels, organized them into seven classes, applied augmentation, and compared transfer-learning models including Xception, Inception V3, VGG16, and VGG19. The best-performing model, Xception, achieved 95.54% test accuracy. Just as importantly, he identified limitations involving dataset size, disease similarity, and generalization. This combination of technical curiosity and reflective problem solving will serve him well in graduate courses and research involving user studies, interaction design, accessible computing, AI-assisted systems, and responsible technology.

In addition to his technical ability, Sakib is a student who has developed steadily over the course of his undergraduate education. His thesis was completed in November 2023, and the result was published in February/March 2024. He communicates respectfully, takes responsibility for his work, and engages seriously with feedback and revision. I believe these qualities will help him contribute positively to a diverse research group and engage seriously with the ethical and social dimensions of AI and interactive technology.

I recommend Md. Sakib for admission with confidence. He combines a CSE foundation, a research experience in applied AI, and approximately three years of professional UX design experience. This combination gives him a valuable perspective for graduate work that connects user research, interaction design, and intelligent systems. I expect him to grow into a capable HCI or human-centered AI researcher and to make meaningful contributions to graduate study.
